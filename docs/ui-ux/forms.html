Inputs

Minimals outlined fields, 56px tall. Labels stay visible above the field and placeholders are examples, never labels. Accept any reasonable format (Postel): trim spaces, ignore case, allow dashes in phone numbers. Errors say how to fix the problem and appear below with aria-invalid and aria-describedby.

Select, dropdown and suggestions

Prefer the native select on mobile: it opens the OS picker. Use the custom dropdown for short lists (7 options or fewer) that need rich rows, or when the native picker would break the look of the form (the blood type). Inside a dialog, which would clip an in-flow menu, the menu goes to the top layer (dropdown-menu with popover) always under its summary, as wide as it, and scrolls inside itself when the list is longer than the room; it closes on a press outside, on Escape (the dialog stays open) and on choosing an option, and the arrow keys move between the options. Group longer lists, and preselect the common choice. When the answer is a name the person types (a rider's username), complete it with suggestions under the field instead of a list to scroll.

Suggestions with an address (open)

When the options are places, each row has a title and under it the address and the distance, in the list item's body. A category row says so and opens its places instead of choosing one. The places the rider saved come first (suggestion-option-saved: their kind's icon at the end of the row, in the muted color of the details, and "Saved" in the details), since they are the likeliest answer; they are matched in the browser, so they are never dimmed while the others load.

Category picker

A short, fixed list of kinds, each with its own icon, picked in one tap (Hick, Miller): a radio group drawn as icon tiles. The icon is the same glyph the saved place wears on the map. Use it instead of a select when there are fewer than a dozen kinds and the picture helps (Jakob, Fitts: the whole tile is the target).

Forms and steps

Split long forms into fieldsets of 3 to 4 fields (Chunking). Fields sit 16px apart and fieldsets 32px apart (Proximity). Fold optional fields in a disclosure (Tesler). In a multi-step flow, say where the person is and how much is left (Goal-Gradient). Set --progress (0 to 1) per instance.
